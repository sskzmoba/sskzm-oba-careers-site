Ask a senior Kazak. Guide a junior one.

From SSB interviews to a second career after service, someone from the school has walked that road before. This is a quiet place for OBA life members to find each other and talk.

How it works

  1. Log in with your OBA emailUse the email the OBA has on record. We send you a 6-digit code; no password to remember.
  2. Say how you would like to take partOffer guidance, ask for it, or both. Pick your areas and how much time you can give.
  3. Find a mentor and send a short requestChoose one quick question, a few conversations, or regular guidance for a few months.
  4. Talk here, at your own paceMessages stay on this site. Share your mobile number only if and when you want to.

Log in

No code after 5 minutes? Check your spam folder, and make sure it is the email registered with the OBA. Not a life member yet? Become one; the mentors circle is open only to life members.

Your privacy

  • Only OBA life members can see profiles or send messages. Nothing here is public.
  • Your email and phone stay private. Others see your name, batch and what you choose to write.
  • Your mobile number is shared only by you, one conversation at a time, and you can hide it again.
  • Pause or leave any time. Leaving deletes your profile and the messages you wrote.
  • Be kind and respectful. Any conversation can be reported to the OBA team, in confidence.

Tips for a good first request
  • Say who you are and where you are now, in two lines.
  • Ask one clear question, for example “Should I take the bank PO offer or wait for the PSC result?”
  • Mentors are volunteers. A quick question is easier to say yes to than a long commitment.
  • Say thank you, and close the conversation when you are done so they can help someone else.
