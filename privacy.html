Privacy

How SSKZM OBA Careers & Mentors handles your information. Written in plain language, in line with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

Who runs this

The SSKZM Old Boys Association (OBA), through its volunteer Technology Cell. Questions or requests: .

The bulletin and sharing a notice

Reading the bulletin needs no login, and we do not track you. When you share a notice, the photo, PDF or text is read by Google's text recognition and Google's Gemini AI to write a summary. The file itself is deleted straight after it is read. The summary is published. If you choose to add your name and batch, they are shown on the post as “Shared by”. Please do not send personal documents or other people's phone numbers.

Your browser keeps a random number and, if you typed them, your name and batch, so you need not retype them and so we can limit misuse. You can clear these by clearing this site's data in your browser.

The mentors circle

Only OBA life members can use it. We check your email against the OBA's member records, and read only your name, batch, roll number and whether you are a life member. What other members see: your name, batch and what you write in your profile. Your email is never shown. Your mobile number is shown only in a conversation where you tap “Share my mobile”.

We keep your profile and messages so the service works, and send you short emails (through Brevo) when someone writes to you. The emails do not contain your messages.

Your choices

You can pause your listing at any time, or delete your profile from “My profile”. Deleting removes your profile and every message you wrote, straight away. Unanswered requests close after three weeks. Login sessions end after 30 days.

To ask what we hold about you, to correct it, or to raise a concern, write to the address above. We will reply within 30 days.
