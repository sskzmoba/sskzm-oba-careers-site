Share a notice

Saw a vacancy, exam date or scholarship in the newspaper or on WhatsApp? Send it here. We read it, check that it is about careers or studies, and put it on the bulletin for every Kazak.

What would you like to send?

Show my name with the post (optional)

If you fill this in, the post will say “Shared by” with your name and batch, for everyone to see. Leave it empty to share without your name.

Choose a photo, a PDF or type the notice first.

What happens after you send

  1. Our system reads the photo or file and writes a short summary.
  2. It checks that the notice is about jobs, exams, studies or careers. Anything else is left out.
  3. If it is new, it appears on the bulletin within a minute. If someone already shared it, nothing is added twice.

Your photo or file is not kept after it is read. Please do not send personal documents or phone numbers.
